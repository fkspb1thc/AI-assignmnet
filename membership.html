WHO CAN JOIN?

MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS.

You do not need a high game rank to join. We value teamwork, respect and a willingness to learn.

  • Be a current APU student.
  • Have an interest in esports and team activities.
  • Respect other members and practise fair play.
  • Follow the club rules and event guidelines.
  • Balance gaming with academic responsibilities.
